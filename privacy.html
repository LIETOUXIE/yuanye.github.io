一、概述

《可波世界》（以下简称“本游戏”）是一款买断制单机养成游戏，支持智能手表与手机。开发者Yuanye（以下简称“我们”）尊重并保护你的个人信息。本政策说明本游戏会处理哪些数据、如何使用、以及你享有的权利。

二、我们不收集什么

  • 本游戏不要求你注册账号、不收集你的姓名、手机号、邮箱、身份证件信息。
  • 不读取通讯录、短信、相册、通话记录。
  • 不采集你的精确地理位置。
  • 本游戏不包含广告，也不接入广告、用户画像、行为追踪类第三方统计 SDK。

三、保存在你设备本地的数据

游戏存档与设置默认保存在你自己的设备本地，不会上传到我们自建的服务器。我们也没有自建的用户数据库。

卸载游戏或清除应用数据会同时删除上述本地数据，此操作不可恢复。

四、Google Play Games 服务

本游戏接入了 Google Play Games 服务（以下简称 GPGS），用于提供云存档、成就与排行榜功能。仅在你主动登录 GPGS 时才会使用。

  • 登录时，Google 会向本游戏提供你的玩家标识（Player ID）与展示名称，用于关联云存档与排行榜名次。
  • 游戏进度会通过 GPGS 上传至你的 Google 云端存档空间，以便在另一台设备上继续游玩。
  • 解锁成就会写入你的 Google 游戏成就记录。
  • 上述数据由 Google 按照其自身的隐私政策存储与处理。我们无法获取你的 Google 账号密码，也不会单独留存可识别你身份的账号资料。
  • Google 隐私政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy

你可以随时在 Google Play Games 或 Google 账号设置中解除与《可波世界》的关联、清除云存档数据；不登录 GPGS 时，本游戏仍可完全离线游玩。

五、局域网联机与设备配对

本游戏支持手机与手表之间的配对、局域网联机功能。该功能仅在你与另一台设备处于同一网络、并主动发起连接时启用，传输内容为游戏状态与操作指令，通过局域网直连完成，不经过我们的服务器，也不用于识别你的身份。

六、设备权限

  • 网络访问：用于 Google Play Games 云存档、成就、排行榜，以及局域网联机。
  • 存储 / 应用数据：用于保存本地游戏存档与设置。
  • 蓝牙：用于在游戏内完成局域网联机与手表配对，仅在本地设备之间传输游戏数据，不用于定位或追踪。
  • 通知：用于在宠物饥饿时提醒你回来喂食，你可以随时在系统设置中关闭。
  • 震动：游戏会在合适的时机提供适当的震动反馈，不采集任何数据。

七、儿童隐私

本游戏面向全年龄段用户，不会有意收集 13 周岁以下儿童的个人信息。由于我们不收集可识别身份的资料，家长如对儿童使用本游戏有疑问，可通过下方邮箱联系我们。

八、数据安全

本地数据受你设备的系统安全机制保护；云存档数据由 Google 按其安全标准保护。我们不会将上述数据出售、出租或提供给任何第三方用于营销。

九、你的权利

  • 随时停止使用并卸载本游戏，删除全部本地数据。
  • 在 Google 账号中撤销本游戏的 GPGS 授权、删除云存档。
  • 如有数据方面的疑问，可通过下方邮箱联系我们。

1. Overview

"Kebo World" (the "Game") is a paid, offline pet-raising game available on smartwatches and phones. The developer, Yuanye ("we"), respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the Game handles, how it is used, and what rights you have.

2. What we do NOT collect

  • No account registration; we do not collect your name, phone number, email address or identity documents.
  • No access to contacts, SMS, photos or call logs.
  • No collection of your precise location.
  • The Game contains no ads and integrates no advertising, profiling or behavioral-tracking SDKs.

3. Data stored locally on your device

Save data and settings are stored locally on your own device by default and are not uploaded to any server operated by us. We do not maintain a user database.

Uninstalling the Game or clearing app data will delete this local data permanently.

4. Google Play Games services

The Game uses Google Play Games services ("GPGS") for cloud save, achievements and leaderboards. These features are used only when you choose to sign in to GPGS.

  • When you sign in, Google provides the Game with your player ID (Player ID) and display name so that cloud saves and leaderboard entries can be linked to you.
  • Your progress is uploaded to your Google cloud save space so you can continue on another device.
  • Unlocked achievements are written to your Google Play Games achievement record.
  • This data is stored and processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy. We never receive your Google password and do not retain identity-linked account profiles of our own.
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You may unlink the Game and delete cloud save data at any time in Google Play Games or your Google account settings. The Game remains fully playable offline without signing in.

5. Local-network pairing and multiplayer

The Game supports pairing between a phone and a smartwatch and local-network multiplayer. These features activate only when you start a connection with another device on the same network. The data exchanged is game state and input commands, sent directly over the local network, never through our servers, and never used to identify you.

6. Device permissions

  • Network access: for Google Play Games cloud save, achievements, leaderboards and local-network multiplayer.
  • Storage / app data: to store local save files and settings.
  • Bluetooth: to run in-game local-network multiplayer and watch pairing. Only game data is exchanged between your own devices; it is never used for location or tracking.
  • Notifications: to remind you to feed your pet when it is hungry. You can turn this off in system settings at any time.
  • Vibration: the Game provides gentle vibration feedback at appropriate moments and collects no data from it.

7. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because we do not collect identifying information, parents with questions may contact us at the address below.

8. Data security

Local data is protected by your device's security mechanisms; cloud save data is protected by Google's security standards. We do not sell, rent or provide this data to any third party for marketing purposes.

9. Your rights

  • Stop using the Game and uninstall it at any time to delete all local data.
  • Revoke GPGS authorization and delete cloud saves in your Google account.
  • If you have questions about your data, you can reach us at the address below.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated with new versions of the Game. Updated content will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued play constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
